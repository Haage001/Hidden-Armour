# Hidden Armour

**Hidden Armour** is a lightweight Fabric mod that gives you full control over the rendering of armor, elytra, nametags, enchantment glint, and selected mob equipment.  

## Key Features

- 🔒 **Toggle Player Armor**  
  Show or hide armor rendering on players at the press of a button.

- 🧭 **Scope Modes (All / Self / Others)**  
  Choose whether hidden armor applies to everyone, only yourself, or only other players.

- 🎚️ **Opacity Slider**  
  Use Opacity (0-100%) instead of a strict on/off look while armor hiding is active.

- 👕 **Per Piece Toggles** <br>
  Toggle exactly which armor pieces are hidden.

- 🪶 **Include/Exclude Elytra**  
  Decide whether elytra should be hidden together with armor.

- ✨ **Disable Enchantment Glint**  
  Toggle the enchantment glint effect on tools and 2D items.

- 🏷️ **Hide Nametags**  
  Toggle the visibility of nametags.

- 🛡️ **Shield Visibility Control**  
  Hide or show shields on player renders (non-blocking state).

- 🐴 **Mob Equipment Controls**  
  Hide or reveal equipment on horses, wolves, and nautilus.

- 🎃 **What Won’t Be Hidden**  
  Heads, carved pumpkins, and other non-armor items will still render normally.

- ⚙️ **In-Game Configuration**  
  Accessible via hotkey or ModMenu.

## Controls

- **Toggle Armor:** `J`  
- **Open Settings:** `U`  
- Both keybinds can be remapped in **Settings → Controls**.

## Commands

- `/hiddenarmour enable|disable|status`
- `/hiddenarmour elytra enable|disable|status`
- `/hiddenarmour scope all|self|others|status`
